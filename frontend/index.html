How to present this system

01

Risk, not certainty

Outputs are AI-generated landslide risk probabilities, not declarations that a landslide will occur.

02

Geographic validation

The final ensemble achieved approximately 0.806 ROC-AUC under 5-fold geographic cross-validation.

03

Prototype threshold

0.30 is an OOF-selected prototype operating threshold, not an official disaster-warning threshold.

04

Operational validation next

Deployment with live IMD/sensor feeds and field validation is required before real-world warning use.
